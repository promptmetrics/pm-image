# Remix Evaluation — PromptMetrics Paper Brand

Three before/after test cases that exercise the brand remix rules in `references/promptmetrics-brand-guide.md`.

## Test Case 1 — A/B testing prompts

### Before (generic Nano Banana prompt)

A modern flat illustration of A/B testing two LLM prompts. Split screen with two glowing chat bubbles labeled A and B, abstract neural network background, bright blue and purple gradients, clean tech aesthetic, friendly 3D icons, upbeat SaaS marketing style.

### After (PromptMetrics Paper remix)

Editorial diagram on warm cream paper background `#f4efe7`. Center headline in Fraunces serif: "Why *testing* beats vibes" with "testing" in italic coral `#d97757`. Two rounded cards with 18px radius and warm hairline borders, labeled A and B in JetBrains Mono, each containing a prompt block and a small bar chart. A thin coral arrow loops from the loser back to a diamond "Stop?" node, then to "Ship". Kicker above in uppercase coral JetBrains Mono with a 24px leading rule: "Step 1 · Golden set · 20–30 real conversations that went wrong". Lucide-style thin icons only. No emoji, no gradients, no glow, no 3D. Cream and ink palette with coral as the only accent.

## Test Case 2 — AI cost spikes

### Before (generic Nano Banana prompt)

A 3D isometric illustration of an AI cost spike. A shiny rocket launching from a laptop, piles of gold coins, bar chart going up exponentially, futuristic city skyline, neon green and electric blue accents, bold sans-serif headline "Unlock AI ROI" with sparkle emoji, cinematic lighting.

### After (PromptMetrics Paper remix)

Warm cream paper background `#f4efe7`. A simple editorial scene: an operator at a minimal desk with a laptop showing a dashboard. Left side: a descending line graph labeled "Token price −92%" with small coin icons. Right side: a rising bar chart labeled "AI spend +16×" in muted coral tints. In the middle, speech bubbles cascade from "user speech" into multiple "API-call" bubbles showing hidden retry chains and reasoning. Headline in Fraunces serif: "Falling token prices won't save the budget you now own". Labels and numbers in JetBrains Mono. Four small pill buttons at bottom: "Diligence", "Discernment", "Description", "Delegation". Thin Lucide-style icons. No rockets, no coins, no neon, no emoji, no 3D.

## Test Case 3 — AI agent failure modes

### Before (generic Nano Banana prompt)

A futuristic hero image showing five AI agent failure modes. Glowing holographic cards floating in space, circuit patterns, robot head icons, cyberpunk color palette with cyan and magenta, dramatic lighting, 3D glassmorphism, bold uppercase headline "AGENT FAILURE MODES".

### After (PromptMetrics Paper remix)

Warm cream paper background `#f4efe7`. Five equal-height cards in a horizontal row, each with 18px radius, cream fill `#efe8db`, warm hairline border, and a thin 3px coral top-bar. Each card contains a single Lucide-style thin line icon and a title in Fraunces serif: "Infinite Loop", "Drifting Compass", "Deadlock", "Ballooning Cost Graph", "Broken Observability". A small coral dot sits in the upper right of each card, and three coral dots form a footer strip. A muted arrow weaves behind the cards to suggest a sequence. A small uppercase JetBrains Mono kicker above reads "Five failure modes" with a 24px leading coral rule. No gradients, no glass, no 3D, no glow, no uppercase headline, no emoji.

## Test Case 4 — Proving AI ROI in six weeks

### Before (generic direction)

A hero image for a blog post about proving AI ROI. Glowing upward arrow, 3D rocket, dashboard with neon charts, excited team celebrating, bright gradient background, bold headline "Unlock Your AI ROI!" with sparkle effects.

### After (PromptMetrics Paper remix)

Editorial diagram on warm cream paper background `#f4efe7`. Top: Fraunces serif headline "Close the *measurement* gap" with "measurement" in italic coral `#d97757`. Below, two horizontal 18px-radius cards with warm hairline borders `#ddd3c4`. Left card labeled BEFORE in uppercase JetBrains Mono, 50% width ink bar, captioned "19% slower in trials (METR)". Right card labeled AFTER, 35% width ink bar, captioned "Real logged delta". A thin coral arrow runs from the BEFORE card through a diamond node marked "Baseline → Run → Math" in JetBrains Mono, then to the AFTER card. Underneath, a six-week timeline as five small rounded pills: Week 0 "Pick process", Weeks 1-2 "Log old way", Weeks 3-4 "Track new way", Weeks 5-6 "Convert to dollars". Bottom right: a small cream spreadsheet card with three column headers in JetBrains Mono: "Time per task", "Error rate", "Volume". Lucide-style thin line icons only. No emoji, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no stock photography. Muted ivory and ink palette with coral as the only accent.
